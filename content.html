On a

Budget

LOW BUDGET, JUST VIBES

Synopsis

On a Budget is a variety show where I have my friends
play different games and challenges that are budget friendly.
Each season has a different theme (every month),
and each episode is a different game (every Friday)!
We try to make it fun and entertaining while still
keeping it simple and easy to watch.

MyGoals

The goal of this show is to create a comfort watch that feels
like hanging out with friends. I want to make viewers laugh and
enjoy the games while building a consistent series.

What I do

I plan the episodes, film the content, edit the videos,
and design the thumbnails by myself!

    Gratitude

    Garden

    INTERACTIVE DIGITAL GARDEN FOR GRATITUDE

    Synopsis

    Gratitude Garden is an interactive digital garden where users can
    add their gratitude messages and watch the public garden grow.
    Each entry becomes part of the visual experience, turning gratitude
    into a space that feels positive and alive.

    MyGoals

    The goal of this project is to encourage reflection and emotional
    expression through interaction. I want users to feel calm and
    connected while exploring gratitude in a creative way.

    What I do

    I design the visuals, build the interaction, and develop the experience by myself!

    Today's

    Thankfulness

    FIND SOMETHING YOU'RE THANKFUL FOR EACH DAY

    Synopsis

    These days people are starting to be ungrateful.
    They either compare themselves or what they have with other people.
    With this content, I want to spread positivity and help
    everyone can upload their daily lives.
    So I created #todaysthankfulness mentioning one thing I'm thankful for today.
    I also want to spread this to my followers by randomly
    asking them what they're thankful for that day.

    MyGoals

    The goal of this content is to spread positivity and gratitude.
    I encourage people to be grateful for what they have.
    It also works as a self-reminder for myself as well.

    What I do

    I film the content, edit the videos, and design the thumbnails by myself!
